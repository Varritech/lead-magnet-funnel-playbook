# 5-Email Nurture Skeletons (7 Days)

Each email has one job and one link. Keep each under 120 words. Replace the [brackets].

Sender: a real person, not "noreply". Plain text style works best.

---

## Email 1: Deliver the guide (Day 0, send right away)

**Subject:** Your [guide name] is here

Hi [first name],

Here is your guide: [download link]

Start with [chapter or step]. It takes [time] and gives you [first win].

Reply and tell me what you are working on. I read every reply.

[Your name]

---

## Email 2: One quick tip (Day 1)

**Subject:** One thing to do today

Hi [first name],

Most people skip [tip topic] and it costs them [pain].

Do this today:
1. [Step one]
2. [Step two]
3. [Step three]

It takes [time]. More in the guide: [link]

[Your name]

---

## Email 3: Story or result, then the offer (Day 3)

**Subject:** How [reader type] got [result]

Hi [first name],

[Two or three sentences: a short real story or result. No made-up numbers.]

The guide gets you started. If you want to skip the setup, I made [low-ticket offer name]. It gives you [what they get].

[Link to offer]

[Your name]

---

## Email 4: Answer the top objection (Day 5)

**Subject:** "[Top objection]?"

Hi [first name],

A common question: "[objection in the reader's words]"

Short answer: [answer in 2 to 3 sentences].

If that was holding you back, here is the next step: [link]

[Your name]

---

## Email 5: Last reminder (Day 7)

**Subject:** Last note on [offer or topic]

Hi [first name],

This is my last email in this series.

Quick recap:
- You have [guide name]
- The next step is [offer name]: [link]

If it is not for you, no problem. Reply with what you would like to see next.

[Your name]

---

## Rules for the sequence

1. One job per email. One link per email.
2. No fake urgency. Do not invent deadlines.
3. Send from a real mailbox so replies reach you.
4. After the series, move buyers to a customer list and non-buyers to your normal newsletter.
5. Track the offer link so you can count purchases from leads per ad.
