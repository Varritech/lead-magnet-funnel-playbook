# Landing Page and Thank-You Page Outline

Use this for a free guide funnel. Use a strong page as a layout reference, then fill in these blocks with your own words, colours and imagery. Never copy logos, images or text.

## Before you start

1. Write the one-line positioning: "This guide is NOT for X. It IS for Y."
2. Find 2 to 3 top landing pages you like. Note the section order and spacing as a reference. Write your own words, colours and imagery. Never copy their logos, images or text.
3. Design for the phone first. Most Meta traffic is mobile.

## Landing page sections

| # | Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headline | Name the reader and the result. "The 12-point Meta ads audit for agencies" |
| 2 | Sub-headline | One sentence on what they get and how long it takes |
| 3 | Cover image | A clean mockup of the guide |
| 4 | Form | **Email only.** One button: "Send me the guide" |
| 5 | What is inside | 3 to 5 bullets, each one a result |
| 6 | Who it is for | 2 to 3 lines. Also say who it is not for |
| 7 | Proof | Real results only. No fake counters or fake scarcity |
| 8 | Second form | Same form again at the bottom |

## Form rules

- Email only to start. Each extra field costs you leads.
- Add a phone field only if you will call or text the lead.
- Put a short privacy line under the button.
- On submit, fire the Lead event (see `checklists/tracking-checklist.md`).

## Headline formulas

- "[Result] for [specific reader] without [pain]"
- "The [number]-point [thing] for [specific reader]"
- "How [specific reader] [does result] in [short time]"

## Thank-you page sections

| # | Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirmation | "Check your inbox" plus the download button right on the page |
| 2 | Next step | One line on what to do first with the guide |
| 3 | Low-ticket offer | One offer, one button, one price shown |
| 4 | Short proof | One real result or quote |
| 5 | No menu | Remove nav links so the only click is the offer |

## Low-ticket offer checklist

- Solves the very next problem the guide raises
- Quick to use (a template, a pack, a short session)
- Price is an easy yes next to the free guide
- Clear promise of what they get and when
- Tracked as its own Purchase event

## Test before you launch

1. Open the page on a phone.
2. Submit the form with a test email.
3. Confirm the guide arrives and the thank-you page loads.
4. Run the tracking checklist.
