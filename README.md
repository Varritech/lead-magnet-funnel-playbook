# How to Build a Lead Magnet Funnel with Facebook Ads

To build a lead magnet funnel, you need five parts: a narrow free guide, a landing page with a short email form, a Meta ad that optimizes for Lead, a thank-you page with a low-ticket offer, and a 5-email nurture over 7 days. Keep the form short and the guide narrow. Judge your ads by purchases from leads, not by cost per lead alone.

This playbook is free. It comes from funnels we run for clients at Varritech. It is written for marketing agencies and in-house marketers, and the templates work in any AI tool.

Results we've hit on client campaigns with this method:

| Result | Cost |
|---|---|
| Free guide download | $0.82 per guide download |
| Name and phone lead | $3.08 per lead |
| Call scheduled on the website | $4.93 per website-scheduled lead |

A lead is a free signup, not a sale. Your niche, offer and price will move these numbers.

## How do I build a lead magnet funnel?

Follow these seven steps in order.

1. **Pick a narrow lead magnet.** One problem, one reader.
2. **Build a landing page** using a strong page as a layout reference. Ask for an email only.
3. **Run a Meta ad** set to optimize for the Lead event.
4. **Show a low-ticket offer** on the thank-you page.
5. **Send 5 emails in 7 days.**
6. **Check tracking** before you spend.
7. **Judge ads by purchases from leads.** Switch to purchase optimization only when you have volume.

The rest of this page explains each step.

## What makes a good lead magnet?

A good lead magnet matches your positioning. It is not a broad category.

Broad guides attract the wrong people. They get downloads and no buyers. A narrow guide attracts people who already want what you sell.

Use this test: write one line that says "This product is NOT X. It IS Y." Then make the guide about Y.

| Weak (broad) | Strong (narrow) |
|---|---|
| "Social media tips" | "How a 5-person agency cuts client reporting from a day to an hour" |
| "Marketing checklist" | "The 12-point Meta ads account audit for agencies" |
| "Email guide" | "The 5-email welcome sequence for a new retainer client" |

### Which lead magnet ideas work for agencies?

1. **A checklist** the reader can finish in 15 minutes (audit, launch, QA).
2. **A swipe file** of ads, subject lines or hooks, grouped by use.
3. **A template pack** (reporting template, onboarding doc, proposal).
4. **A short guide** of 6 chapters or fewer that fixes one problem.
5. **A calculator or scorecard** that gives the reader a number.

Pick the format you can make in a day. Short and specific beats long and general.

## How do I build the landing page?

Do not design from scratch. Use a strong page as a layout reference. Write your own words, colours and imagery. Never copy logos, images or text.

Rules:

- Ask for **email only**. Fewer fields means cheaper leads.
- Put the headline, a cover image and the form above the fold on mobile.
- Use a button that says what they get ("Send me the guide").
- Add 3 to 5 bullets of what is inside.
- Show proof you can show honestly. No fake counters.

## How do I lower cost per lead with Facebook ads?

Four things move cost per lead most. Fix them in this order.

1. **Make the ask small.** Test the smaller ask first: a free guide for an email only. A form that also asks for a phone number is a bigger ask, so test it against the short form before you commit.
2. **Optimize the ad set for the Lead event.** Not traffic. Not link clicks.
3. **Make simple static ads.** One point per ad. Name the person the guide is for in the headline.
4. **Send every ad to the same page** and let Meta find the best pair. Wait three days before you judge.

If a bigger ask gets no leads, shrink it. Remove paid steps and extra fields until people say yes, then add one back at a time.

## What should the thank-you page offer?

A low-ticket offer. This is often called a tripwire.

The thank-you page is the best place for it. The reader just said yes and is paying attention.

How to do it:

1. Deliver the guide first. Put the download or link at the top.
2. Show one low-ticket offer under it. It should be a small buy that solves the next problem the guide raises.
3. Make it one button. No menu, no other links.
4. Keep the price low enough that it is an easy yes.
5. Say what they get and how fast.

The offer should be a natural next step, not a trick. If the guide is a checklist, the offer could be the done-for-you template pack.

## What should the email sequence look like?

Five emails in seven days. Each email has one job and one link.

| Email | Day | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Deliver the guide, point to the first win |
| 2 | 1 | Teach one quick tip from the guide |
| 3 | 3 | Share a short story or result, show the low-ticket offer |
| 4 | 5 | Answer the top objection |
| 5 | 7 | Last reminder with a clear next step |

Skeletons you can paste are in [`templates/emails.md`](templates/emails.md).

## How do I check that tracking works?

Before you spend anything, check that Meta receives the Lead event.

- The Lead event fires **on form submit**, not on page load.
- You tested it in a **real browser**, not only a testing tool.
- A **server-side** event is sent as well, with the same event ID so Meta does not count it twice.

The full list is in [`checklists/tracking-checklist.md`](checklists/tracking-checklist.md).

## How do I judge the ads?

Cost per lead is a start. It is not the goal.

1. Count **purchases from leads** per ad. This is the number that matters.
2. A cheap lead that never buys is a bad lead. An expensive lead that buys is a good one.
3. Keep optimizing for Lead while volume is low.
4. Switch the ad set to **purchase optimization** only once you have enough volume, meaning a steady flow of purchases each week. Meta needs data to learn. Switching too early starves the ad set.

## FAQ

### What is a lead magnet?
A free thing you give in exchange for an email address. Common ones are guides, checklists, templates and swipe files.

### What is a tripwire offer?
A low-ticket offer shown right after someone opts in. It gives a free lead an easy first purchase.

### How many fields should my lead form have?
As few as you can. Email only is the best start. Add a phone field only if you will call or text, and expect a higher cost per lead.

### How many emails should the nurture have?
Five over seven days is a good start. Each email has one job and one link.

### Should I optimize for Lead or Purchase?
Start with Lead. Move to Purchase when you have a steady flow of purchases. Until then, judge ads by purchases from leads.

### Does this only work with Meta ads?
No. The funnel, the page and the emails work with any traffic. The tracking steps in this repo are for Meta.
